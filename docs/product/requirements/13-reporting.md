# Requirement Specification 13 — Operational Reporting & Analytics

> **Capability Namespace:** `reporting_analytics`  
> **Requirement Standard:** IEEE 830 / ISO 29148 Standard Specification Language  
> **Target Application:** Talnova Onboarding Platform

---

## 1. Functional Requirements

### REQ-REP-001: Operational HR Dashboard
* **Description:** The system shall provide real-time onboarding status dashboards for HR Administrators.
* **Specification:**
  * Metrics shall include: Active onboarding count, average time-to-productivity (days), overdue task count, milestone sign-off bottlenecks, and course completion drop-off rates.

### REQ-REP-002: eNPS & Confidence Survey Scoring
* **Description:** The system shall aggregate employee self-confidence scores and eNPS onboarding satisfaction surveys.
* **Specification:**
  * Reports shall display score distributions by department, location, and manager.
