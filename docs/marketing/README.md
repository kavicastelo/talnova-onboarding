## 1. Executive Mission & Positioning Architecture

Talnova Onboarding is the unified enterprise operating system for modern employee onboarding, frontline enablement, and digital compliance. By bridging learning delivery (LMS), cross-departmental operations, event-driven workflow automation, and deskless touch terminals, Talnova transforms corporate onboarding from a scattered administrative burden into a high-velocity retention and productivity engine.
