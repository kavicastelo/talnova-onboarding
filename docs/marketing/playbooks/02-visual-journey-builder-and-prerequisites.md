# Visual Journey Builder & Prerequisite Gatekeeper: Marketing Playbook & Asset Brief

## 🎯 Executive Summary & One-Liner
* **The Hook:** Stop overwhelming new hires with 50 tasks on Day One—build sleek, interactive roadmaps that unlock sequentially as they learn, achieve, and grow.
* **The Elevator Pitch:** Talnova's Visual Journey Builder empowers People Ops leaders to design stunning, multi-phase onboarding roadmaps in minutes. With drag-and-drop ease and intelligent prerequisite gating (`prerequisiteStepId`), new hires experience an intuitive, gamified progression that eliminates cognitive overload and boosts Day-30 retention.

## 💪 Superpowers (Core Strengths)
* **Structured Cognitive Pacing (Clarity & Focus):** Prerequisite step-locking shields new hires from day-one paralysis. By unlocking Stage 2 only when Stage 1 compliance is sealed, employees always know their exact next priority, replacing panic with calm momentum.
* **Modular Drag-and-Drop Canvas (Speed for People Ops):** Seamlessly stack digital e-signatures, interactive LMS courses, IT provisioning tickets, and manager 1-on-1s into an elegant timeline in under 10 minutes without touching a line of code.
* **Real-Time Roadmap Visibility (Autonomy & Confidence):** Gives employees a transparent, visual flight-map of their onboarding journey from Day 0 to Day 90. Progress bars, unlock animations, and phase milestones turn mandatory onboarding into an engaging quest.

## ⚔️ The Competitive Edge (vs. Status Quo & Competitors)
* **The Old Way / Competitor Approach:** Legacy onboarding dumps a massive, unprioritized checklist or 10 separate email invites on Day One. New hires scramble to figure out what's urgent vs. what can wait, leading to missed compliance deadlines and early disengagement.
* **Our Disruptive Difference:** A modular, visual roadmap builder with strict dependency enforcement. Modules, courses, and tasks unlock dynamically only when required precursors (e.g., Security Clearance or Code of Conduct e-signature) are successfully completed and verified.
* **The Kill-Shot Comparison:**

| Capability Dimension | Traditional Checklists / Notion Docs | Monolithic Enterprise LMS | Talnova Journey Builder |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pacing & Sequencing** | Static, unguided list; all visible at once | Rigid SCORM courses; disconnected from operational tasks | Intelligent prerequisite gating; dynamic multi-phase unlocks |
| **Content Unification** | Links out to 8 different external tools | Training only; no HR documents or IT tasks | Single unified container: LMS, tasks, e-sign, milestones, and check-ins |
| **Authoring UX** | Clunky forms or unmanaged doc pages | Complex, bureaucratic LMS administrative panels | Drag-and-drop visual timeline builder with live preview |
| **New Hire Experience** | Intimidating wall of text; high drop-off | Corporate, drab compliance portal | Interactive, milestone-driven visual roadmap with gamified progress |
| **Adaptive Logic** | None; static for every employee | Course assignment rules only | Tailored journeys dynamically resolved by department, role, and location |

## 🚀 The "Magic Moment" (How Users Master It)
* **Activation Step:** HR Admin opens `/journeys/new`, names their template (e.g., "Global Engineering Onboarding - EMEA"), and drags three sequential phases onto the canvas: "Phase 1: Welcome & Compliance", "Phase 2: Dev Environment Setup", and "Phase 3: First Pull Request & Buddy Pairing".
* **The AHA! Moment:** The new hire logs in on morning one. Instead of an overwhelming 40-item list, they see a clean, welcoming timeline: Phase 1 is glowing active with a crisp 3-step checklist, while Phase 2 displays an elegant lock icon with the label *"Unlocks upon Security Clearance"*. Stress vanishes instantly.
* **Pro-Tips for Power Users:** Combine cross-role dependencies—set an IT Admin task (e.g., "Issue AWS Production Tokens") as the prerequisite trigger before the Employee's "First Deployment" module unlocks.
