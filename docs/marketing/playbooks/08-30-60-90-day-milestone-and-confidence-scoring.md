# 30-60-90 Day Milestone Reviews & Confidence Scoring: Marketing Playbook & Asset Brief

## 🎯 Executive Summary & One-Liner
* **The Hook:** Stop waiting for annual reviews to discover an employee is struggling—align performance and confidence at Day 30, 60, and 90 before bad habits form.
* **The Elevator Pitch:** Talnova's 30-60-90 Day Milestone Engine replaces ambiguous probationary periods with structured, two-way performance alignment. Combining employee self-confidence ratings, manager rubric evaluations, and formal sign-off gates, it creates an objective roadmap to full role productivity and crushes early ramp-up ambiguity.

## 💪 Superpowers (Core Strengths)
* **Two-Way Expectation Calibration (Mutual Transparency):** Compares employee self-ratings directly against manager assessments across key competencies (role mastery, tooling, cultural integration). Discrepancies are highlighted instantly, preventing blind spots.
* **Structured Ramp-Up Progression (Clear Velocity):** Breaks the daunting first 3 months into bite-sized 30-day horizons: Day 30 (Absorb & Learn), Day 60 (Contribute & Collaborate), Day 90 (Lead & Deliver).
* **Formal Governance & Audit Readiness (Peace of Mind):** Streamlines probation sign-offs, performance documentation, and compliance handover with timestamped manager approvals and tamper-evident records.

## ⚔️ The Competitive Edge (vs. Status Quo & Competitors)
* **The Old Way / Competitor Approach:** Managers conduct vague, unstructured check-ins over coffee or skip 30-day reviews altogether. By month three, both employee and manager realize they were operating on completely different expectations, often resulting in expensive early terminations.
* **Our Disruptive Difference:** A systematic, dual-perspective evaluation rubric built into the core onboarding lifecycle with automated schedule reminders, side-by-side score comparison visuals, and formal approval gates.
* **The Kill-Shot Comparison:**

| Feature Dimension | Informal 1-on-1 Coffee Chats | Legacy Annual Review Tool (Lattice/15Five) | Talnova 30-60-90 Day Milestone Suite |

## 🚀 The "Magic Moment" (How Users Master It)
* **Activation Step:** On Day 28, an automated trigger alerts the employee to submit their **Day 30 Milestone Self-Rating** inside `/milestones`.
* **The AHA! Moment:** The employee rates their confidence across 5 pillars (e.g., Codebase Comfort: 4/5, Deployment Process: 2/5). The manager receives an alert, opens their review portal, and sees the side-by-side radar: The manager had rated Deployment Process as 4/5, but notices the employee feels insecure. In their Day 30 sync, instead of generic small talk, the manager spends 20 minutes walking through deployments, erasing the employee's anxiety on the spot.
* **Pro-Tips for Power Users:** Set journey prerequisite gates on Day 60 milestones—automatically prevent progression to advanced solo projects until the Day 30 foundational milestone is formally reviewed and marked `APPROVED`.
