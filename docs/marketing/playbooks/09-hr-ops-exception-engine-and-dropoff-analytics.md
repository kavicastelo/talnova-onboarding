# HR Ops Exception Engine & Real-Time Drop-off Analytics: Marketing Playbook & Asset Brief

## 🎯 Executive Summary & One-Liner
* **The Hook:** Stop guessing why new hires stall—pinpoint onboarding drop-offs, SLA bottlenecks, and compliance risks in real-time before they impact business velocity.
* **The Elevator Pitch:** Talnova's HR Ops Exception Engine and Drop-off Analytics dashboard gives People leaders total operational telemetry over every active onboarding cohort. By exposing step-by-step funnel drop-offs, highlighting overdue task SLAs, and tracking live eNPS sentiment, HR transforms from a reactive support desk into an agile, data-driven talent powerhouse.

## 💪 Superpowers (Core Strengths)
* **Funnel Drop-Off Pinpointing (Root Cause Diagnostic):** Visualizes the new hire journey as a continuous conversion funnel. Instantly isolates the exact lesson, quiz, or document where new hires get stuck or abandon progress.
* **Proactive Exception Triage (SLA Enforcement):** Aggregates cross-organization blockers into a unified "Exceptions Console": Overdue manager check-ins, delayed laptop shipments, and unsigned NDAs are prioritized for immediate resolution.
* **Live Cohort Health & eNPS Intelligence (Executive Polish):** Delivers board-ready metrics on onboarding completion velocity, average time-to-productivity, and new hire satisfaction scores filtered across departments and global locations.

## ⚔️ The Competitive Edge (vs. Status Quo & Competitors)
* **The Old Way / Competitor Approach:** HR relies on static end-of-quarter spreadsheets or hears about onboarding failures only after an employee quits. There is zero visibility into operational bottlenecks while onboarding is actually happening.
* **Our Disruptive Difference:** Continuous, real-time funnel telemetry combined with an active exception engine that alerts People Ops the moment a milestone is breached or an onboarding task stagnates.
* **The Kill-Shot Comparison:**

| Analytics Dimension | Traditional HRIS Reporting | Standalone Survey Tool (Qualtrics) | Talnova HR Ops Exception & Analytics Engine |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Data Recency** | Batch export, often weeks old | Periodic snapshot after survey closes | Live real-time event streaming and instant metric updates |
| **Funnel Granularity** | High-level status ("Active" vs "Complete") | No operational funnel tracking | Step-by-step conversion and drop-off rate analysis across journey phases |
| **Operational Triage** | Manual hunting through individual profiles | None | Automated Exception Console flagging overdue SLAs and blockers |
| **Cross-Silo Visibility**| HR data only (no IT or manager data) | Feedback scores only | Unified view across HR compliance, IT provisioning, and Manager evaluations |
| **Cohort Segmentation** | Basic department sorting | Manual tagging | Dynamic slicing by department, role, location, manager, and hire cohort |

## 🚀 The "Magic Moment" (How Users Master It)
* **Activation Step:** The VP of People logs in and navigates to `/analytics` and `/hr-ops/exceptions`.
* **The AHA! Moment:** The **Drop-off Funnel Chart** instantly highlights a sudden 42% bottleneck on "Step 4: Cloud Security Compliance Quiz" for the Sales cohort. Clicking the bottleneck reveals the quiz passing threshold was accidentally set to 100% instead of 80%. In the **Exception Console**, three overdue manager evaluations in the Dublin office are flagged in red. With two clicks, HR resolves the quiz threshold and fires an automated reminder to the Dublin managers. The bottleneck dissolves within an hour.
* **Pro-Tips for Power Users:** Set up automated webhook alerts when a cohort's average 30-day completion rate dips below 85%, routing diagnostic notifications straight to the designated HR Business Partner on Slack.
